# Privacy policy – Family wallboard

Family wallboard is a private, self-hosted wall display used by one family. It runs on a Raspberry Pi in the family's home.

**What it accesses.** When a family member signs in with Google, the wallboard requests read-only access to their Google Calendar (`calendar.readonly`) and their e-mail address (to show which account is connected).

**How the data is used.** Calendar events are shown on the family's own wall display. Nothing else is done with them.

**Where the data is stored.** Only on the family's own Raspberry Pi: the sign-in token and a cache of upcoming events. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party, and there is no analytics or advertising.

**Removing access.** Disconnect the account in the wallboard's Settings, or remove "Wallboard" at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

**Contact.** Open an issue at <https://github.com/muggsyM/wallboard/issues>.
